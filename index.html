The Heartbeat of Alberton

At Ted Smokers KO9, we are proud of our Gauteng roots. We established our home in Alberton to give locals a unique space where culinary craft and underground music culture collide.Our smoking process is a labor of love, requiring time, precision, and a passion for flavor. We bring that exact same dedication to our music events, hosting local talent and house music purists who know how to move a crowd. We are a family of foodies, music lovers, and good-vibe creators. Step into Ted Smokers KO9, where everyone is welcome, the meat is always tender, and the music never stops.
